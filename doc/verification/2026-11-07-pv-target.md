# A target of -1.0 at 88 does not move a PV run; +1.0 is followed by its stop

2026-11-07. Two store charges on a surplus written to 74, to see whether
88, `PV-Zielwert (Smartfox und Solar-Log)`, is the export the PV controller
holds (`doc/pv-control.md`, "The PV target at 88").

The first run wrote 74 as a meter would read it with 2.0 kW of PV and the
pump the only load, from the first write on, and was to step 88 after 12
minutes. It never reached a step: once the start program ended, the speed
climbed to the top and held it for 100 s after the mean of 74 had turned
negative, then fell, and the compressor stopped after 8 minutes, before the
minimum runtime.

The second run held 74 at 0 from the compressor's start. With the speed
steady at 50.4 rps, 88 at -1.0 moved nothing in 3 minutes. 88 at +1.0 was
followed 40 to 50 s later by a fall to the floor and a stop, 10 minutes
21 seconds after the start, so within seconds of the minimum runtime's end;
the run alone cannot tell which of the two ended it. The second start came
30 minutes after the first run's stop, to the second.

## What was set

As on 2026-11-06 (`doc/verification/2026-11-06-pv-surplus-own-draw.md`):

## What the web pages showed

- **12763** listed 74 and 88 as written, to two decimals, beside a `GLT
Value` of the same rounded to one: -1.00 and 1.00 for 88. Not the mean of
  74, and not 74 plus the draw.
- **10263**, the PV page, printed 74 as written as `Überschuss`, as on
  2026-11-06, and nothing that follows 88. `mom./prog. Leistung Heizen` read
  0.7 kW before the first run and 0.8 from its start on, through the second.

## What it shows

- **88 at -1.0 does not move a running charge.** For 3 minutes, 6 to 9
  minutes after the start and past the start program, the speed held 50.4
  rps, as it did under 88 at 0 before. Neither a jump, which a proportional
  term on 88 would give, nor a ramp, which an integral would, came.
- **88 at +1.0 was followed by a fall and a stop**, but the fall began 10 to
  20 s before the minimum runtime of 10 minutes, setting 5412, ran out, and
  the stop came 21 s after, so the run does not show which ended it. On the
  morning of 2026-11-06 0 at 74 held 120 rps for about 66 s with a mean of
  exactly 0, about 3 minutes past the minimum runtime, which argues for 88;
  that run held the top rather than the start program's speed.
- **If 88 acts, it acts on one side.** -1.0 and +1.0 do not mirror each
  other. A target clamped at 0, so that the controller never draws from the
  grid on its own account, would fit both.
- **The climb of the first run is the controller's, not the compressor
  warming up.** In both runs the start program held 50.4 rps for about 4
  minutes. In the first the speed then climbed to the top on a positive
  mean; in the second, with the mean at 0, it held 50.4 for nearly 6
  minutes more. The 4 kW at the top is the draw at full speed, as on the morning
  of 2026-11-06.
- **A meter-style 74 overshoots also without a stale start.** The mean held
  the start's surplus this time as a meter would, 2.0 less the draw, and the
  speed still went from the start program to the top and the run stopped
  before the minimum runtime. Every change of speed reaches the controller
  through `PV011`'s two-minute mean, so it lags the draw by up to two
  minutes.
- **How long the top holds once the mean turns differs.** 100 s here, with
  the mean down to -1.65; on 2026-11-06 the speed fell 10 s after the mean
  crossed zero in the evening and 20 s after -0.5 was written in the
  morning. A PI controller on the mean of 74 does not explain the three
  alike.
- **A PV start is held off for 30 minutes after a PV stop.** The second
  request came 29 min 59 s after the first run's stop, with 2.0 standing at
  74 for the 20 minutes past the standstill of 5419. On 2026-11-06 the
  second start, written for from 9 to 19 minutes after the stop, did not
  come, which fits. Which setting holds the 30 minutes no source names.
- **A start takes a minute from the request.** 1100 read `Ein` 61 and 60 s
  after 1090 and 1091 moved, in both runs.

## What the runs do not settle

- Whether 88 at +1.0 or the end of the minimum runtime stopped the second
  run. A run with 74 and 88 at 0 held past 10 minutes, and one with +1.0
  written about 5 minutes after the start, would.
- Whether 88 below 0 does anything at all, at another time of the run or for
  the start.
- What sets the 30 minutes before a new PV start.

## Reproducing

With the PV menu as above, at Kunde level for Modbus:

    cabal run idm-dump -- HOST --watch 10 74 88 1008 1090 1091 1100 1074 4122 1004 1098 > watch.jsonl
    IDM_PIN=... cabal run idm-web -- HOST --enter-fachmann
    IDM_PIN=... cabal run idm-web -- HOST --watch 10 6245 10263 12763 > webwatch.jsonl
    tools/drivepv.py HOST watch.jsonl --final -0.5 --final-target 0 \
      2.0-4122/20/ein+720/0 2.0-4122/20/ein+1140/1.0 \
      2.0-4122/20/ein+1560/-1.0 2.0-4122/20/ein+1620/0 > write-1.log
    tools/drivepv.py HOST watch.jsonl --final -0.5 --final-target 0 \
      2.0/20/ein/0 0/20/ein+360 0/20/ein+540/-1.0 0/20/ein+720/1.0 \
      0/20/ein+780/0 > write-2.log
    tools/runtable.py watch.jsonl --web webwatch.jsonl --writes write-1.log
    tools/runtable.py watch.jsonl --web webwatch.jsonl --writes write-2.log --from 12:13:00

The first schedule ends at the stop, before its second phase. The tables'
mean is `tools/runtable.py`'s, but for the first two rows of the first run,
which it does not print without a write log covering the window, and the
first of the second run, taken from the first run's final -0.5.
